Hi! I'm a software engineer who writes about systems, tooling, and the craft of building software.

## How this site works

This blog runs on **InkGit**. Every article is a Markdown file in a Git repository:

- Writing happens in Markdown/MDX.
- Publishing is a Git commit.
- History, review, and backups come from Git itself.

Your content belongs to you. Your repository is your CMS. Your website is your presentation layer.
